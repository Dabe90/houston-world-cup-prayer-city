The story of who you become

Class of David 1024 is not a course you finish. It is a road you walk with a class, one day and one semester at a time — until God's Word lives in you.

Take your seat
  1. 01

    You arrive

    You come hungry

    Busy, curious, longing for more of God. You say yes, take your seat in the class, and the journey begins in October.

  2. 02

    Knowledge

    You learn His Word

    Monday to Friday you read with your class. The Bible stops being a book you own and becomes a voice you know.

  3. 03

    Revelation

    You begin to see

    Every Tuesday at 10:00 PM WAT you pray together. What you read starts to open up — you hear what God is saying to you.

  4. 04

    Experience

    You walk it out

    Assignments, honest feedback from your coordinator, and a class that notices when you are missing. What you know becomes how you live.

  5. ★

    July 2028

    You finish complete

    Four semesters later you leave a complete disciple — filled with God's knowledge, revelation and experience, ready to help others walk the same road.

Questions, answered

Semester 1 has started. Can I still join?

Yes. Semester 1 began on 5 October 2026 and enrollment is still open. Register, start with this week's reading, and your coordinator will help you settle in.

How long is the class?

Two years, in four semesters, from October 2026 to July 2028 — with a Christmas break, a mid-term week, and a long holiday in August and September.

What happens each week?

A scripture reading every weekday, Monday to Friday, in your portal. Class prayer every Tuesday at 10:00 PM WAT. Assignments from your coordinator, with marks and feedback.

What do I need to register?

Your name, email address and a password. Your phone number is optional. After you register you go straight into your student portal.

I want to serve as a coordinator. How do I apply?

Use Request access. Your account is reviewed, and your coordinator portal opens once you are approved.

What happens to my details?

We use your details only to run Class of David 1024 and to contact you about the class. We do not sell or share them.
