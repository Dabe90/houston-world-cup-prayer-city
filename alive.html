A letter, not a flyer

This is not another indoor program with plastic chairs and a long altar call.

Alive Hangout is the day we step outside — gold-hour light, good food, honest laughter — and remember who we are: alive in Christ, and dead to sin.

“Likewise reckon ye also yourselves to be dead indeed unto sin, but alive unto God through Jesus Christ our Lord.” Romans 6:11 · KJV

Beloved,

You were not saved to live a dim Christian life. You were saved to shine. Once in this season we gather outdoors — brothers, sisters, marriages, and singles — for games, special presentations, guest ministers, food, and dance that honors Jesus.

We teach righteousness. We teach sexual purity. We stay away from every defilement. We make ourselves ready for our Saviour. Fun is not the opposite of holiness here. Fun is how a holy people celebrate that they are free.

— Dear Daughter Bible Study Group Nigeria

The atmosphere

Picnic air. Holy gathering. Real joy.

Sun on the grass, food on the table, the Word in the open — this is what Alive Hangout feels like. Our house photos, plus the kind of outdoor fellowship we are curating.

Who it is for

Four streams. One hangout.

Come as you are — and leave more awake. This is family air, not a dating marketplace.

Men

Brotherhood without the boys-club compromise. Strength that stays clean. Fellowship that makes you want to live right when the field goes dark.

Women

Sisterhood with taste and backbone. Beauty that does not bargain with the world. A table where your dignity is not up for debate.

Married

A date-day with a holy assignment. Laugh together. Sit under the Word together. Go home remembering why covenant is a gift.

Singles

Community that does not treat singleness as a waiting room for sin. Joy, friendship, and a standard that keeps you ready for Jesus.

The experience

A curated outdoor day — not a random picnic.

Think executive hospitality meets revival fire. You will play. You will eat. You will hear the Word. You will dance. You will leave lighter and cleaner.

Games that actually make people laugh

Outdoor contests, team challenges, and the kind of play that turns strangers into family before the first sermon starts.

A table that feels like home

Food is part of the ministry. Come hungry. Stay for seconds. Nobody should remember the Word on an empty stomach.

Dance before the Lord

Joy with a spine. Movement that worships. The world does not own celebration — we take it back outdoors, in the light.

Guest ministers & special presentations

Voices that carry fire, and presentations that stay with you after the sun goes down. This is not filler. It is the hinge of the day.

Why we gather

Alive in Christ. Dead to sin. Ready for Jesus.

The hangout is the wrapping. The teaching is the gift. We will not dilute this — not for culture, not for applause, not for a crowd.

  1. 01

    Alive in Christ

    You are not trying to become alive. In Him, you already are. We teach you to live like resurrection is true on a Monday, not only on a Sunday.

  2. 02

    Dead to sin

    Grace is not permission. We reckon the old life finished — no secret life, no double life, no “small” compromise we dress up as weakness.

  3. 03

    Righteousness as a lifestyle

    Not a mood. Not an aesthetic. A people who look like their Father in how they speak, spend, date, marry, and serve.

  4. 04

    Sexual purity & no defilement

    We teach the body as holy ground. Stay away from porn, fornication, unclean talk, and every mixture that soils the wedding garment.

  5. 05

    Ready for our Saviour

    The point is not a nice outdoor photo. The point is a church that can look up and not be ashamed when Jesus appears.

How to show up

Dress the day. Honor the field. Bring a willing heart.

Modest outdoor elegance — clothes you can move in, a light wrap for evening air, shoes that can handle grass. Take the photo, then put the phone away and be present.

Bring

Water bottle, a friend who needs this, a heart that is done pretending. Leave the “maybe later” at home.

Leave

The costume of church without consecration. Alive Hangout is a getaway — and a mirror. Come ready to be both.

The practicals

Everything a serious guest asks before they say yes.

When and where is it?

Saturday, 12 June 2027, outdoors in Nigeria. The venue is released only to people who complete registration.

Do I have to register?

Yes. Registration is compulsory. Only registered names will receive the venue and admission on the day. Walk-ups will not be taken. Register everyone in your party.

Who can register?

Men, women, married couples, and singles. Believers who want a holy outdoor day — and friends you are praying into the Kingdom. Bring them. Register them.

Can I come with my spouse or a friend?

Yes. Put the number in your party on the form, and make sure every person is registered. Married couples, come together if you can. Singles, do not come alone if someone in your life needs this air.

Will there be teaching on relationships and purity?

Yes. That is not a side note. We teach righteousness and sexual purity, and we call the house to stay away from any defilement so we are ready for Jesus Christ.

I want to serve, not only attend.

Tick “I want to help host” on the form. Welcome, food, games, and ushering will need willing hands. We will follow up as the day gets closer.
