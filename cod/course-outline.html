Course outline

Each year in Class of David equals one month of intensive formation. You will receive assignments through your student portal as coordinators publish them.

Assignment workflow

How tasks reach you

  1. Coordinators draft assignments and send them to the President for template formatting.
  2. Published tasks appear on your student dashboard (naming: Y1-A01-topic).
  3. You submit before the due date; coordinators mark work and forward marked copies to the President.
