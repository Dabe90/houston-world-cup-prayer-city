The story

More than a class — a refining fire

Four graduating sets. Hundreds transformed. One vision: intimacy with Jesus that changes how you live.

Class of David began four years ago as a whisper from God — mentors gathered, months of waiting, then a clear purpose: raise a generation who know God deeply, live disciplined lives, and carry His presence into the world. What started with curiosity became conviction. Timid hearts became bold. Shy voices learned to evangelize. Lives were pruned, stretched, and refined.

Graduates speak of discipline, consistency, and commitment — of reading Christian literature daily, submitting tasks, praying through the night, and encountering God on the mountain top in a way they had never known before. Many were baptized in the Holy Spirit. Many found clarity for the next season of their lives. Not everyone who begins finishes — but those who stay graduate as glory-carriers.

What to expect

Rigorous — on purpose

We tell you upfront so no one is surprised. This is real discipleship, not a casual Bible club.

9 months

Weekly Bible study, Christian literature, daily tasks, prayer rhythms, and spiritual accountability — October through graduation.

Daily discipline

Bible reading, prayer, and assigned books (including classics like God’s Generals) — consistency that rewires your walk with God.

Mountain-top night

An annual encounter many graduates call unforgettable — praying until God’s presence is tangible, even meeting virtually across the world.

Evangelism

Speak to people about Jesus with boldness. Many who arrived shy left preaching the Gospel with confidence.

Mentors & coordinators

Teaching, counsel, and prayer from leaders who have walked this path — meet the mentors including President Abe Damilola and the Dear Daughter team.

Honest warning

Not everyone who starts finishes. Come determined. Come hungry. Come ready for God to do a thorough work in you.
